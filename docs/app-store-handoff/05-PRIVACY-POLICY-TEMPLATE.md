## Information we collect

Depending on your account role and the features you use, we may process:

- Account and contact information, such as name, email address, phone number, profile image, role, and authentication records.
- Booking and studio information, such as classes, coaches, reservations, cancellations, attendance, and check-in records.
- Membership and transaction information, such as membership cards, usage records, orders, prices, currencies, payment status, and refunds.
- Content you provide, such as reviews, profile details, uploaded images, and body or fitness measurements you choose to record.
- Administrative and security records, including role-based actions, audit logs, request identifiers, timestamps, and basic technical logs.

## Payments

Payments may be processed by Stripe and supported payment partners. Payment credentials such as full card numbers and CVC values are handled by the payment provider and should not be stored by Good Vibe Pilates & Yoga. We may receive transaction identifiers, payment method type, amount, currency, country, status, and refund information.

## Camera and photo library

Authorized staff may use the camera to scan booking check-in QR codes. Administrators may select images to manage coach profiles, studio content, or products. Access occurs only after device permission is granted.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, enforce role permissions, provide bookings and memberships, process transactions, support users, operate the studio, prevent misuse, maintain audit records, and comply with legal obligations.

## Sharing

We may share information with service providers that operate the app, including hosting, database, storage, notification, and payment providers. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party behavioral advertising unless this policy and the app are updated to disclose that practice.
