## Common requests

- Cannot sign in: confirm the email, password, and selected role. A role can only be changed after logout.
- Booking or cancellation: open My Bookings and follow the studio's displayed cancellation rules.
- Payment or refund: provide the order number, date, and amount. Never email a full card number or CVC.
- Membership card: contact the studio for freeze, extension, transfer, upgrade, or balance questions.
- Privacy or account data: request access, correction, or deletion at `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`.
